The tracked-out uppercase mono label that titles every Atempo panel.

```jsx
<EyebrowLabel tone="accent">WHERE YOU LEFT OFF · 2H AGO</EyebrowLabel>
<EyebrowLabel>OPEN THREADS · 2</EyebrowLabel>
```

Always uppercase, always mono, `letter-spacing: 0.16em`. Use `accent` tone for the recap's "where you left off" label; `muted` for the rest.
